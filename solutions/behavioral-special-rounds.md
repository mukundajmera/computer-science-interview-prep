# 🧪 Special Behavioral Rounds

Coach answer outline for recruiter screens, cross-functional rounds, deep dives, follow-ups, and team matching.

---

## 1) Recruiter Screen

### Goal
Pass qualification and communicate fit quickly.

### Winning approach
- 60-90 second TMAY tuned to role
- 2 stories ready: impact + conflict/ambiguity
- Ask process-smart questions (rubric, round types, expectations)

### Avoid
- Overly technical deep dives
- Generic "I can do anything" positioning

---

## 2) Cross-Functional Round

### Goal
Show collaboration, alignment, and influence quality.

### Winning approach
- Explain stakeholder map and competing goals
- Show negotiation mechanics and decision hygiene
- Highlight communication adjustments by audience

### Avoid
- Framing partners as blockers
- Pure engineering-only narrative

---

## 3) Leadership/Philosophy Round

### Goal
Reveal decision principles, not slogans.

### Winning approach
- State your principle
- Provide concrete case where principle guided action
- Discuss trade-off and boundary conditions

### Avoid
- Abstract values with no example
- "Always/never" answers without context

---

## 4) Project Deep Dive / Retrospective

### Goal
Demonstrate judgment under scrutiny.

### Winning approach
- Start with problem, constraints, and success criteria
- Walk critical decisions and alternatives
- Cover failures, mitigations, and what changed after

### Avoid
- Perfect-story narrative with no mistakes
- Architecture-only answer without execution detail

---

## 5) Follow-Up Round

### Goal
Address prior concerns and reduce hiring risk.

### Winning approach
- Assume the round is risk-targeted
- Bring stronger evidence in previously weak areas
- Be explicit about lessons and changed behavior

### Avoid
- Repeating unchanged stories
- Defensive posture about prior feedback

---

## 6) Team Matching Conversation

### Goal
Mutual fit on problem space and operating style.

### Winning approach
- Map your strengths to team charter and current challenges
- Ask about success metrics, ownership model, and collaboration patterns
- Share what environment helps you perform best

### Avoid
- Treating it as a one-way sales pitch
- Ignoring team-specific context

---

## Universal 30-Second Pivot Template
When interrupted:
1. "I can go deeper on technical trade-offs or stakeholder dynamics."
2. "My key contribution was __ under constraint __."
3. "The measurable result was __, and the follow-on change was __."
