# 🧭 Leadership Deep Dive (Behavioral)

Coach answer outline for leadership-focused rounds (senior IC + manager tracks).

---

## Interview Objective
Demonstrate leadership beyond task execution:
- Direction setting under ambiguity
- Cross-team influence
- Decision quality under constraints
- People and execution health

---

## Core Prompt Types
- "Tell me about a strategic decision you led."
- "Describe a time you influenced without authority."
- "How have you developed people or raised team standards?"
- "Tell me about a failed initiative and what changed after."

---

## Strong Story Structure (4-5 minutes)
1. **Business context:** Why this mattered now
2. **Scope and stakeholders:** Teams, dependencies, risk
3. **Leadership action:** Alignment, trade-offs, sequencing
4. **Execution control:** Milestones, risk management, pivots
5. **Outcome + second-order effects:** Metrics + org impact
6. **Leadership reflection:** What you changed in your approach

---

## Senior IC Emphasis
- Architecture choices linked to business risk
- Influence across product/platform/security/ops
- Raising engineering standards through systems, not heroics

### Example IC framing
- "I aligned three teams on a migration plan by defining interface contracts, readiness gates, and rollback ownership."
- "Result: incident rate decreased 42% and onboarding time for new services dropped by two weeks."

---

## Manager Emphasis
- Team design, delegation model, and prioritization
- Coaching and performance management outcomes
- Execution predictability and stakeholder trust

### Example manager framing
- "I split a broad initiative into two streams with distinct owners, introduced weekly risk reviews, and reset goals with product leadership."
- "Result: we recovered schedule slippage and improved roadmap confidence in subsequent quarters."

---

## High-Value Follow-up Responses
- **On trade-offs:** "I chose predictability over feature breadth because trust recovery was the critical objective."
- **On conflict:** "I separated disagreement about approach from shared outcome goals, then aligned on measurable exit criteria."
- **On people impact:** "I codified decision frameworks so the team could repeat the behavior without escalation."

---

## Red Flags To Avoid
- Inflated scope without concrete ownership
- "Command-and-control" framing with no collaboration evidence
- Strategy claims without operational details
- Success claims with no measurable outcomes

---

## Closing Pattern
- "The key leadership lesson was converting ambiguity into a shared execution model with explicit risk ownership."
- "If I ran it again, I'd invest earlier in dependency mapping to reduce churn."
