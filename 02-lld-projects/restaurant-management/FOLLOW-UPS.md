# Restaurant Management - Follow-Up Questions

## Follow-up questions
1. How would you prevent two hosts from reserving the same table for the same time slot?
2. What order modifications are allowed after an order reaches the kitchen?
3. How would the kitchen queue prioritize orders during peak hours?
4. How would you split a bill by equal shares versus selected order items?
5. How would ingredient availability affect menu item availability and order submission?

## Variants and extensions
1. Walk-in waitlist with table-size matching.
2. No-show and cancellation handling for reservations.
3. Kitchen display notifications when orders become ready.
4. Staff assignment by section and table load.

## Interviewer probes to prepare
- "What is the source of truth for table availability?"
- "Which order transition commits work to the kitchen?"
- "Where do tax, tip, split, and payment status live?"
