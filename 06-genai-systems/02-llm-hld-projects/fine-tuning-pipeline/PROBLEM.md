## 1. Problem Statement

Design a Fine-Tune-as-a-Service platform for Large Language Models (LLMs), focusing specifically on Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA). The platform must handle data curation, job scheduling, distributed checkpointing, evaluation gating, adapter registry, hot-swapping, and robust tenant isolation with precise GPU-hour costing.
