# AI Coding Interviews Quiz

24 questions focused on AI-enabled coding interview execution, format strategy, codebase orientation, verification, and communication.

Time: 35 minutes
Difficulty: Medium-Hard

---

### Question 4

D) Discussing trade-offs

### Question 5

A) Prompt creativity only

### Question 6
