# Ride Sharing — Follow-Up Questions

## 1. How do we add pooled rides?
Add seat-capacity constraints, detour thresholds, and multi-passenger route optimization.

## 2. How do we support multi-city expansion?
Partition operational control by city/region while sharing identity and billing primitives.

## 3. How do we improve ETA accuracy?
Use road-network routing plus live segment speeds and periodic model recalibration.

## 4. How do we handle peak demand supply gaps?
Blend surge multipliers, wait queues, and proactive driver incentives.

## 5. How do we detect fraud/safety anomalies?
Stream trip telemetry into rules + ML scoring for GPS spoofing and route anomalies.

## 6. How do we support multi-region outages?
Introduce region-local failover modes with degraded feature flags.
