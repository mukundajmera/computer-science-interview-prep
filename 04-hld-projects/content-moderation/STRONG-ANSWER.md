# Content Moderation — Strong Answer Outline (Time-Boxed)

Say: "I will design moderation as a risk pipeline with clear action thresholds, appeal paths, and feedback loops, not as a single classifier that silently decides everything."

## 0-6 min: Requirements
- Clarify modalities, policy goals, and precision guardrails.

## 6-12 min: Estimation
- Compute event throughput + harmful prevalence + review budget.

## 12-20 min: API + decision model
- Define evaluate/report/appeal contracts and decision lifecycle.

## 20-34 min: Architecture
- Walk ingestion, scoring, policy action, review queue, and feedback paths.

## 34-42 min: Deep dives
- Threshold calibration, class imbalance, drift/adversarial adaptation.

## 42-45 min: Wrap-up
- Re-state operating point and why it fits business harm constraints.
