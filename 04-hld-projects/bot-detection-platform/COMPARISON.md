# Bot Detection Platform — Comparison

## vs Content Moderation Platform

| Aspect | Bot Detection | Content Moderation |
|---|---|---|
| Decision unit | account/session | content item |
| Primary features | behavior, graph, device | text/media/context |
| Main enforcement | challenge/throttle/suspend | demote/review/remove |

## vs Rate Limiting

| Aspect | Bot Detection | Rate Limiting |
|---|---|---|
| Decision basis | model-driven risk | fixed quota/fairness rules |
| Adaptation | campaign-aware | mostly threshold-based |
| Output | account risk actions | request-level allow/deny |
