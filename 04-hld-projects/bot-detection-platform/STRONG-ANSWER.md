# Bot Detection Platform — Strong Answer Outline

Say: "I will treat bot detection as an adversarial platform: collect multi-signal evidence, score risk in real time, keep humans in the feedback loop, and measure false positives as carefully as abuse blocked."

## Suggested Interview Narrative

1. **Framing:** adversarial objective and user-trust guardrails.
2. **Estimation:** high event throughput + low label density.
3. **API/Data model:** risk, confidence, reasons, and action timeline.
4. **Architecture:** signal ingest -> dual-path scoring -> calibrated remediation.
5. **Deep dives:** remediation ladder, campaign adaptation, investigator queue.
6. **Wrap-up:** abuse suppression vs collateral damage trade-off.
