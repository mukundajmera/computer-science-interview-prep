# Bot Detection — Follow-Up Questions

## 1. How do we detect bot campaigns, not just single accounts?
Add campaign graph clustering and shared infrastructure fingerprints.

## 2. How do we avoid model poisoning via appeals?
Separate trusted investigator labels from user-provided signals with weighting controls.

## 3. How do we support region-specific policy?
Apply policy versioning by jurisdiction with local enforcement bands.

## 4. How do we reduce compute cost at peak abuse spikes?
Introduce tiered scoring and cache reusable risk features.

## 5. How do we evaluate detection quality with scarce labels?
Use stratified sampling, delayed outcome proxies, and periodic gold audits.

## 6. How do we handle account takeovers vs synthetic accounts?
Split remediation policies and feature families by abuse subtype.
