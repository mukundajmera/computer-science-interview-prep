## 🎯 How to Use Cheatsheets

### During Study
- Use as quick reference while learning concepts
- Verify your understanding against the reference
- Fill in gaps in your knowledge

### Before Interview
- Review 1-2 hours before the interview
- Focus on areas you're weakest in
- Memorize key numbers (latency, storage)

### During Mock Practice
- Keep open during timed practice
- Use to verify your design choices
- Build muscle memory for decision-making
