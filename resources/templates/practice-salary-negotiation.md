# 🎯 Salary Negotiation Practice Template

> **Type:** Offer-stage compensation negotiation simulation  
> **Total Time:** 45 minutes  
> **Flagship Scenario:** Senior/Staff IC offer with multi-component package

---

## ⏱️ Time-Boxed Structure

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|------|------|------|
| 1. Offer intake | 5 min | Capture package details and clarify constraints |
| 2. Position assessment | 8 min | Define leverage, target range, and fallback |
| 3. Stakeholder routing plan | 5 min | Decide what to discuss with recruiter vs hiring manager |
| 4. Counter narrative draft | 10 min | Build a consistent, component-prioritized ask |
| 5. Counter-loop simulation | 12 min | Practice 2-3 likely response scenarios |
| 6. Closing plan | 5 min | Decide acceptance conditions and written confirmation checklist |

### Requirements and constraints

- Keep a single coherent storyline throughout the drill.
- Prioritize total compensation and role fit, not only base salary.
- Include both Global and India adaptation notes for your scenario.
- Avoid legal interpretation; include explicit "verify local rules" caveats.
- End with written-close criteria before final acceptance.

---

## 📊 Scoring Rubric

| Dimension | 1 — Weak | 2 — Below Avg | 3 — Meets Bar | 4 — Strong | 5 — Exceptional |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| Preparation quality | No target or fallback | Partial prep | Defined target + fallback | Clear ranges + rationale | Evidence-backed plan with scenario branches |
| Stakeholder routing | Wrong channel usage | Inconsistent routing | Mostly correct routing | Correct routing with rationale | Precise routing + relationship protection strategy |
| Ask quality | Vague ask | Single-lever ask only | Basic component ask | Prioritized component ask | Data-backed, trade-off-aware ask sequencing |
| Narrative consistency | Contradictions | Minor drift | Mostly consistent | Fully consistent | Consistent under pressure + pivots |
| Trade-off handling | Rigid stance | Limited flexibility | Some lever swaps | Smart lever swaps | Optimized swaps aligned with constraints |
| Risk management | No risk checks | Partial checks | Basic risk checks | Strong risk controls | Proactive boundary handling and professional close |
| Closing discipline | Premature accept/escalate | Weak close | Adequate close | Clear written-close checklist | High-signal close with documented terms |

**Scoring:** 28+ = Strong Hire Signal · 21-27 = Hire Signal · 14-20 = Needs Rework · <14 = Rebuild fundamentals

---

## ✅ Self-Review Checklist

### Preparation
- [ ] I captured every package component accurately.
- [ ] I set a target range, acceptable range, and walk-away boundary.
- [ ] I identified evidence for each ask.

### Stakeholder routing
- [ ] I kept compensation negotiation routed through recruiter.
- [ ] I used hiring-manager conversations for role and scope signal only.
- [ ] I avoided contradictory narratives across channels.

### Component strategy
- [ ] I prioritized component asks (primary/secondary/fallback).
- [ ] I prepared at least one base-light alternative path.
- [ ] I included timeline and joining constraints where relevant.

### Communication quality
- [ ] I led with appreciation and role intent.
- [ ] I stated asks clearly and concisely.
- [ ] I stayed calm and non-adversarial during pushback.

### Risk handling
- [ ] I handled "best and final" professionally.
- [ ] I avoided unverifiable claims.
- [ ] I confirmed final terms in writing before acceptance.
