## 6) ✅ Self-Review Checklist

- [ ] I can state the primary signal in one sentence.
- [ ] My selected story is high scope for my level.
- [ ] My actions are specific and use clear ownership language.
- [ ] My results include measurable or operational evidence.
- [ ] My learning section shows behavior change, not generic reflection.
- [ ] I can adapt this story for at least 3 different signal prompts.
