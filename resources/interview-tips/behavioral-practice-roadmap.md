# 🏋️ Behavioral Practice Roadmap

A progressive plan to move from content familiarity to pressure-tested interview performance.

---

## Overview

Practice quality matters more than practice volume. Use staged pressure: controlled solo rehearsal, adaptive AI drills, peer mocks, and high-signal mock rounds.

---

## Requirements and constraints

- Maintain 8-12 tagged stories (ownership, conflict, ambiguity, growth, leadership).
- Use a fixed scorecard for every session.
- Practice under timeboxes and interruption.
- Re-drill weaknesses within 24-48 hours.
- Track both IC and manager variants for core stories.

---

## Scoring rubric

| Dimension | 1 - Weak | 3 - Solid | 5 - Strong Signal |
|------|------|------|------|
| Structure control | Rambling | Mostly structured | Consistent CARL delivery under pressure |
| Signal fit | Misses signal | Partial match | Precise signal targeting |
| Follow-up resilience | Breaks under probing | Handles basic probing | Defends decisions and adapts cleanly |
| Ownership language | Ambiguous | Mixed clarity | Consistent clear ownership |
| Reflection depth | Generic | Specific learning | Behavior change with mechanism |

---

## Common pitfalls

- Practicing only full-script recitation.
- Skipping follow-up stress tests.
- Collecting feedback without targeted re-drills.
- Rotating too many weak stories instead of strengthening anchors.
- Practicing alone without realistic interruption dynamics.

---

## Follow-up questions

- Which signal area is still weakest after three sessions?
- Which story collapses under trade-off probing?
- What evidence is missing in your top two stories?
- Which answer repeatedly overruns time?
- What changed after the last debrief?

---

## Variants and extensions

- 10-day sprint: 3 solo, 3 AI, 3 peer, 1 professional/high-signal mock.
- Weekend drill: big three + one failure story + one leadership story.
- Team prep extension: run pair review where partner scores only ownership and action density.
- Manager track extension: add two org-design and people-development stories.
