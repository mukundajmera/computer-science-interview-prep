## 🎯 General Interview Strategy

### Before the Interview
1. **Review 2-3 systems** you're most comfortable with
2. **Practice drawing diagrams** quickly and clearly
3. **Memorize key numbers** (latency, QPS, storage estimates)
4. **Prepare questions** to ask the interviewer

### During the Interview
1. **Listen carefully** and take notes on requirements
2. **Ask clarifying questions** before designing
3. **Think out loud** - share your reasoning
4. **Start simple** then iterate and improve
5. **Discuss trade-offs** explicitly
6. **Don't jump to implementation** too quickly
7. **Use diagrams** and examples to illustrate
8. **Handle feedback gracefully** - it's a collaboration

### After the Interview
1. **Ask about next steps** if not mentioned
2. **Send thank you note** if appropriate
3. **Note what went well and what to improve**

## ⏱️ Time Management

### For LLD (45-60 min interview)

| Phase | Time | What to Do |
|-------|------|------------|
| Requirements | 5 min | Clarify scope, ask questions |
| Core Entities | 5 min | Identify nouns, main objects |
| Relationships | 5 min | Map how entities connect |
| Pattern Selection | 5 min | Choose applicable patterns |
| Class Design | 15-20 min | Attributes, methods, responsibilities |
| Edge Cases | 5-10 min | Validation, errors, concurrency |
| Wrap-up | 5 min | Review, questions |

### For HLD (45-60 min interview)

| Phase | Time | What to Do |
|-------|------|------------|
| Requirements | 5-10 min | Functional + non-functional |
| Estimation | 5 min | QPS, storage, bandwidth |
| API Design | 5-10 min | Core endpoints |
| High-Level Design | 15-20 min | Architecture, data flow |
| Deep Dive | 10-15 min | Specific components |
| Trade-offs | 5 min | Alternatives, follow-ups |

## 📋 Interview Day Checklist

### Technical Prep
- [ ] Review CONCEPTS.md cheatsheet
- [ ] Practice one HLD problem end-to-end
- [ ] Review estimation numbers
- [ ] Review 2-3 LLD designs you know well

### Logistics
- [ ] Test your internet/video connection
- [ ] Have paper and pen ready for diagrams
- [ ] Quiet environment, minimize distractions
- [ ] Water nearby

### Mindset
- [ ] Get good sleep the night before
- [ ] Remember: interviewers want you to succeed
- [ ] It's a conversation, not an exam
- [ ] Deep breaths if you feel stressed

## 💬 Useful Phrases

### Starting the Interview
- "Before I start designing, I have a few clarifying questions..."
- "Let me make sure I understand the requirements correctly..."
- "What's the expected scale we're designing for?"

### During Design
- "Let me think about this out loud..."
- "One approach we could take is..."
- "The trade-off here is..."
- "Given our requirements, I'd choose X because..."

### When Stuck
- "I'm not 100% sure about this, but my thinking is..."
- "Let me step back and reconsider..."
- "Could you give me a hint on which direction to explore?"

### Handling Feedback
- "That's a great point. Let me adjust..."
- "I hadn't considered that. Here's how I'd address it..."
- "You're right, that could be a bottleneck. We could..."
